Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Sunday Book Data Engine is a privately operated application supporting The Sunday Book football intelligence publication.

Google Drive Access

The application requests the Google Drive drive.file permission to create, upload, update, and manage files it creates or that its operator explicitly authorizes.

Information Used

The application uses Google OAuth credentials to authenticate its operator and accesses application-specific Google Drive files for publishing NFL intelligence datasets.

Data Storage and Sharing

OAuth credentials are stored in restricted local files and may be supplied as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets for automated execution. Application-generated files are stored in the operator's Google Drive account. The application does not sell Google user data or share it with advertisers.

Data Retention and Deletion

Generated files remain in Google Drive until removed by the operator. The operator may revoke application access through Google Account settings and delete associated files and credentials.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application operator using the support email displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
